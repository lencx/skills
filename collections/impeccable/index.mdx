<div class="curated-story">
<div class="curated-story__lede">
<p class="curated-story__thesis">Impeccable turns design into a system of authorities and rendered evidence. It is not a collection of prompts that merely asks an agent to make a page look better.</p>
<p>A session starts by resolving PRODUCT.md, DESIGN.md, the matching surface brief, and platform guidance. It then loads exactly one command playbook, and loads a separate craft floor immediately before UI edits. Deterministic detector rules and live browser variants supply evidence that model-only critique cannot.</p>
</div>
<section class="curated-story__section curated-story__signature" aria-labelledby="signature-model">
<header class="curated-story__section-heading">
<h2 id="signature-model">The authority stack behind each UI change</h2>
<p>Each layer answers a different question. Keeping them separate lets a redesign replace visual direction without rewriting product truth, and lets mechanical checks support rather than impersonate design judgment.</p>
</header>
<div class="curated-signature curated-signature--authority-stack" data-story-kind="authority-stack">
<article class="curated-signature__item" style="--story-index:1"><span class="curated-signature__meta">Product truth</span><h3>PRODUCT.md</h3><p>Users, jobs, evidence, operating context, constraints, and accessibility stay durable.</p></article><article class="curated-signature__item" style="--story-index:2"><span class="curated-signature__meta">Visual truth</span><h3>DESIGN.md + surface brief</h3><p>The product-wide visual world and the decisions unique to one surface remain distinct.</p></article><article class="curated-signature__item" style="--story-index:3"><span class="curated-signature__meta">One judgment</span><h3>Command playbook</h3><p>critique, audit, polish, harden, and other commands load only the method needed for the current task.</p></article><article class="curated-signature__item" style="--story-index:4"><span class="curated-signature__meta">Before editing</span><h3>Craft floor</h3><p>A separate quality floor constrains typography, contrast, spacing, iconography, and common design shortcuts.</p></article><article class="curated-signature__item" style="--story-index:5"><span class="curated-signature__meta">Rendered evidence</span><h3>Detector + live browser</h3><p>Deterministic rules catch mechanical defects; live mode compares real rendered alternatives.</p></article>
</div>

<section class="curated-story__section" aria-labelledby="project-mechanisms">
<h2 id="project-mechanisms">Where the method is more specific than “good design”</h2>
<div class="curated-story__mechanisms">
<article><h3>Product truth and visual direction stay separate</h3><p>A redesign may replace the visual world while preserving users, product purpose, evidence, content, and constraints recorded as product truth.</p></article><article><h3>Commands own different judgments</h3><p>critique evaluates experience quality, audit checks technical quality, polish closes shipping gaps, and harden covers errors, i18n, overflow, and edge cases.</p></article><article><h3>Tool-backed rendered evidence</h3><p>Detectors catch mechanical defects, while live mode operates a real preview with selected elements, alternatives, annotations, and accept or reject actions.</p></article>
</div>
</section>
<section class="curated-story__section" aria-labelledby="project-practice">
<h2 id="project-practice">Choose the command by the judgment you need</h2>
<ol class="curated-story__practice">
<li><p>Run impeccable init before other commands so later work has real brand and product context.</p></li><li><p>Pair a command with a concrete target, such as audit the blog, polish settings, or harden checkout.</p></li><li><p>Use critique for experience judgment and audit for deterministic technical checks; do not treat them as interchangeable.</p></li>
</ol>
</section>
<section class="curated-story__section" aria-labelledby="adoption-decision">
<h2 id="adoption-decision">Adopt the method without leaking private context</h2>
<div class="curated-story__decision">
<div>
<h3>Use it for</h3>
<p>Landing pages, portfolios, application UI, and dashboards that need shaping, UX critique, technical audit, or final polish.</p>
</div>
<div>
<h3>Review before sharing</h3>
<p>Installation can add project hooks. Codex users should review and approve them, and may need to approve again after hook definitions change.</p>
</div>
</div>
</section>
